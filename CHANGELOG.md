# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here.
Format inspired by [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/). Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Security
- Anti-bot protection (R-035, `014_anti_robots.sql`): per-IP hourly ceilings in the database (5 profiles, 30 posts, 60 comments or club messages, 300 reactions; adjustable in `reglages.limites_ip`), the IP is never stored (salted sha256 fingerprint, purged after 24 h) and nothing is blocked when it is unknown. « Rejoindre » and « Publier » forms get an invisible trap field and a 3-second minimum: bots get a fake success and nothing is sent. Exact Turnstile activation order in docs/CONFIGURATION.md §3.1. JavaScript budget: 150 KB loaded at start-up per page, 250 KB including on-demand modules.

### Fixed
- E-mail never attached to accounts created while e-mail sending was failing (anonymous auth user without `email` / `new_email`, address only in the profile): « Renvoyer l'e-mail de confirmation » now falls back to the profile address instead of answering « Aucune adresse e-mail à confirmer » without calling the server, and reports sending errors (« L'envoi a échoué, réessaie dans un instant ») instead of ignoring them. Pages also attach the profile address silently, once per browser session. If the address already belongs to another confirmed account, Mon espace says so and nothing is changed. The end-to-end test no longer attaches e-mails (SMTP is live: no mail to invented addresses).
- « Mon espace » on phones: the identity card is now mobile first (avatar + pseudo on one row, pseudo on two lines at most, buttons below at full width; former layout from 768 px), and the page no longer overflows horizontally at 360 px (grid tracks `minmax(0, 1fr)` instead of `auto`, which grew to the width of the referral link). Moderation buttons wrap instead of overflowing; the bottom banner reserves the iPhone home-bar area.

### Changed
- Home hero card (R-034, `013_probleme_du_moment.sql`): « Le problème du moment » is now loaded from the database (public `get_probleme_du_moment()`: the problem of the poor, published, not masked, author not blocked, with the most reactions over the last 7 days, else the most recent; public projection only) and links to its page; the static card stays as a fallback. The word « Exemple » is gone from the site: no more badge on cards, on the story card or on the hero, no more « problèmes d'exemple » sentence on « Les chiffres »; demo addresses are now `membreN@demo.invalid` (older saved demo states are migrated).
- The Rien is now the main unit everywhere (R-032): amounts show « 4x Rien » in big (title font, strong colour) and the euros after, in small (« 4 000 € » / « soit 4 000 € »): wall cards, hero card, problem page, story card, leaderboard (podium and lines), Mon espace, « Les chiffres » (« 83,38x Rien de problèmes… », « 0x Rien financé »), price screen of the rich (« 3x rien par mois » / « 300 € »), moderation; the publish forms show the conversion in big under the amount field. Home: new section « La monnaie officielle de Qui paie ? : le rien » right after the hero (1 rien = 100 €, 1 Rien = 1 000 €, examples as tokens). Pure display function `montantAffiche()` in `riens.js`, unit-tested.
- Problems of the poor (`008_plafond_pauvres.sql`): the amount is now required, a whole number from 1 to 10 000 € (« Un problème de pauvre ne dépasse jamais 10x Rien (10 000 €). »), and the limit goes from 3 to 10 problems per 24 h. The one example above the cap now asks for 9 000 €. Publish form: required amount field, `max=10000`, live conversion in Riens.
- JavaScript budget is now measured per page (entry module + everything it can import, dynamic imports included), still 150 KB, instead of the sum of every module of the site.
- Story card: the excerpt gets fewer lines when the title is long, so the author line never runs into the footer.
- Moderation page, restricted-access screen: a visitor without an account now gets a main « Rejoindre » button (the join dialog stays on the page, which refreshes once the account exists), with « Déjà membre ? Se connecter » as secondary; a member who is not a moderator sees their pseudo, their member id to copy and a hint to ask the administrator.
- Sensitive expressions, v2 (`007_mots_sensibles_v2.sql`): fewer false positives on a humour site. Ambiguous single words (`mourir`, `enceinte`, `abus`, `drogue(s)`, `frappe`) are replaced by explicit first-person expressions (`veux mourir`, `je suis enceinte`, `abuse de moi`, `je me drogue`, `il me frappe`…); `addiction`, `alcoolique`, `pensees suicidaires`, `idees noires` added. « Je vais mourir de rire » and « mon enceinte Bluetooth » are now published at once.
- Immediate publication (`005_publication_auto.sql`): a problem that passes the filters is published at once, so its author can share it within the second (« C'est en ligne ! » screen with share buttons and the story card). Problems containing a sensitive expression (distress, violence, serious illness, precariousness — table `mots_sensibles`) still wait for a human review. `reglages.publication_auto = false` switches back to full pre-moderation.
- Service worker: pages, JavaScript, CSS and the manifest are now network first (cache only offline); fonts and images stay stale-while-revalidate. The cache version is stamped with the commit SHA at deploy time, and an open page reloads once when a new version takes over: no more stale `config.js` after a deploy.
- The live site now runs on the real Supabase backend (`MODE: 'supabase'`). Without a Turnstile site key, anonymous sign-in and magic links are called without a captcha token.

### Added
- « La Gazette des pauvres » (R-043, `021_gazette.sql`, code only, sending enabled later): a weekly e-mail to the members who ticked the box (same consent and unsubscribe link as the announcements) with the top 5 of the week, the poor of the month, the new rich members and the themes of the next week. Frozen issue per week, never sent twice, 250 e-mails per 24 h shared with the announcements. Edge Function `gazette` (moderator or weekly job secret, Brevo), « Aperçu de la gazette » and « Envoyer la gazette » in moderation.html; the weekly pg_cron job is documented in docs/CONFIGURATION.md §4.4, not enabled.
- Per-problem link previews (R-042): 10 share images (1200×630, one per category, generated by `tools/images.mjs`) and an Edge Function `partage` (code only, deployed with `--no-verify-jwt`): link-preview robots get the title of the problem, « 5x Rien recherchés — soit 5 000 € » and the image of its category; humans are redirected (302) to `p.html?id=…`; hidden or unknown problems go to the home page. New `CONFIG.URL_PARTAGE` (empty by default): when set, every share button of a problem uses it. Deployment in docs/CONFIGURATION.md §4.3.
- The duel « Lequel est le plus drôle ? » (R-041, `020_duel.sql`): `duel.html` (« Le duel » in the navigation) shows two random problems of the poor side by side (stacked on mobile); tap the funniest, a short animation, the next duel comes, endlessly. Visitors can play without an account (per-IP ceiling); the voter is kept as a salted fingerprint only. « N duels votés » counter, « Partager ce duel » (replays the same pair), « Les plus drôles de la semaine » ranking; duel wins of the last 7 days count in « Le problème du moment ».
- Follow a profile (R-047, `019_suivis.sql`): « Suivre » / « Suivi ✓ » on the public profile (with the number of followers) and next to the author on the problem page, by share code (never an id; members only, IP quota of the reactions). Mon espace: « Mes pauvres suivis » and « Mes riches suivis » with their last post. Wall filter « ★ Mes suivis ». On-site notification when a followed account publishes. « Les plus suivis » (top 10 poor, top 5 rich) on the leaderboard page and a preview on the home page.
- Public profile « Mon CV de pauvre / de riche » (R-040, `018_profil_public.sql`): `profil.html?c=<share code>` shows the pseudo, camp, arrival date, rank of the month and all-time rank, total in Riens, badges and the member's posts (and the crowns given by a rich member), with « Partager mon CV » and a downloadable story card (« Je suis 12ᵉ pauvre du mois avec 23x Rien — et toi ? »). Pseudos on cards, on the problem page and in the leaderboard link to it. Public `get_profil_public(p_code)`: by share code, never an id nor an e-mail.
- Titles and badges (R-039, `017_badges.sql`): 13 computed badges (🥇🥈🥉 podium of the month, 💸 Roi du découvert, 🏅 Smicard légendaire, 🔟 Plafond atteint, 👑 Couronné, 😂 Star du rire, 🛥️ Vu depuis un yacht, 🎩 Mécène imaginaire, 🤝 Rabatteur, 💬 Pipelette, 🌱 Premier problème), nothing stored. Shown next to the pseudo in the leaderboard and in « Mes badges » (with the ones left to unlock) in Mon espace. Public `get_badges(p_code)` returns pseudo + badges only.
- Seasons of the leaderboard (R-038, `016_saisons_palmares.sql`): a monthly leaderboard (Paris calendar month, reset on the 1st at midnight) next to the all-time one: « Ce mois-ci » (default) / « Depuis toujours » tabs on palmares.html, « 🥇 Le pauvre du mois » on the home page, rank of the month and all-time rank in Mon espace. New `get_palmares(p_limite, p_periode)` / `get_mon_rang(p_periode)`; the old signatures still return the all-time ranking and the site falls back to them until the migration is applied.
- The powers of the rich (R-037, `015_pouvoirs_riches.sql`): an active rich member crowns up to 3 problems of the poor per day (Paris day, one crown per problem, removable): « 👑 Couronné par … » on cards and on the problem page, and a crown weighs 10 reactions in « Le problème du moment ». On-site notifications (« Le riche X a couronné ton problème »): unread count on the header avatar, « Mes notifications » in Mon espace. Two reactions reserved to active rich members, « 🛥️ Je regarde ça depuis mon yacht » and « 🎩 Mon majordome s'en occupe » (everyone sees the counters). Home: « Le riche de la semaine » (most crowns over 7 days, else the newest active rich member, moderators excluded) with their last post. Everything stays hidden until the migration is applied.
- Theme of the day (R-036, no database): one category per day of the week, Paris time (Monday « Mon patron » … Sunday « Mon défi »). Compact line on top of the home page and a full banner above the wall (« Je raconte » opens « Publier » with the category preselected, « Les voir » filters the wall, share the theme); the chip of the day's category is marked; a problem of the day's category is shared as « Thème du jour : … ».
- « Un nouveau riche est arrivé » (`012_annonces_riches.sql`): activating a pending rich member creates a 72 h announcement shown on top of the home page and of the wall (« 🚨 Un nouveau riche vient d'arriver : <pseudo>. Va voir sa première publication. »; public `get_annonces()` returns pseudo + link only). Moderation page: « Annoncer ce riche par e-mail » calls the Edge Function `supabase/functions/annoncer-riche` (code only, deployment in docs/CONFIGURATION.md): moderator check, Brevo API, poor members who ticked the box only, 250 e-mails per 24 h at most, never twice, unsubscribe link on every e-mail (`desinscription.html`, `se_desinscrire`). Join checkbox renamed « Préviens-moi par e-mail quand un riche débarque (et les nouvelles du projet) ». The camp is stored in the user metadata so that the Supabase « Change email address » template can greet rich members differently.
- Problems and posts of the rich (`011_publications_riches.sql`): an active rich member (validated by a moderator) publishes « Un problème de riche » (title, text, optional amount with no 10 000 € cap) or « Une simple publication » (text only, 600 characters) through `publier_riche`; a poor member only publishes problems of the poor, a rich member never does. Same filters, sensitive expressions, reports and moderation. Wall: « Tout / Problèmes de pauvres / Problèmes de riches / Publications de riches » filters (`get_problemes_publics(…, p_type)`); lemon cards with a crown and a « Première publication » badge. Rich problems never count in the leaderboard, « Les chiffres » nor the home counter. Moderation views show the text of a publication (which has no title).
- Paying rich members, simple version (`010_riches_payants.sql`): no rich account is active without a moderator. A new rich account (or a poor member switching to the rich camp) is pending: it can read the site but cannot publish, react, comment, report nor read / write in the rich club (checked in the database by `wp_exiger_membre`). It is shown the prices (300 € per month — 3x rien, 3 000 € per year — 3x Rien) and a single « Contacter le support » button (pre-filled e-mail). Moderation page: new « Riches » tab (activate 1 month / 1 year, deactivate, refuse = back to the poor camp). An expired period puts the account back to pending (computed, no scheduled job). Moderators are always active; the « Riches » counter only counts active rich members. Bottom banner: « Projet humoristique — aucun argent ne circule entre membres ».
- Leaderboard of the poor (`palmares.html`, « Le palmarès » in the navigation, `009_palmares.sql`): top 100 of the poor ranked by the total of their published, visible problems (tie-break: number of problems, then the oldest member), with a humorous podium and the signed-in member's line highlighted even outside the top 100. Mon espace (poor): « Mon total », « Ton rang : 42ᵉ sur 312 pauvres », « Voir le palmarès » and « Partager mon rang ». `get_palmares` / `get_mon_rang` expose only pseudo, camp, number of problems, total and rank.
- The « rien / Rien » unit (1 rien = 100 €, 1 Rien = 1 000 €): every amount is now shown with its value in Riens — wall cards (« 4 250 € recherchés · 4,25x Rien »), problem page (« Pour un riche, ça vaut 4,25x Rien »), story card, « Les chiffres » total and a live conversion under the amount field of the publish form. Pure function `enRiens()` in `riens.js`, unit-tested.
- Mobile moderation page (`moderation.html`, linked from nowhere, `noindex`, never cached): four tabs (to review · published in the last 48 h · reported · masked) with thumb-sized buttons to publish, refuse, redirect, mask / restore and block the author. Backed by `006_moderateurs.sql`: table `moderateurs` and `mod_*` functions that refuse anyone who is not a moderator (no execute right for `anon`) and never return an e-mail address. Simulated in demo mode.
- Demo on demand: `?demo=1` switches the current tab to demo mode (no call to Supabase), with a "Quitter la démo" link and `demo=1` kept in internal links; "Essayer la démo" link in the footer.
- `tools/e2e-supabase.mjs`: end-to-end test against the real Supabase project (join rich/poor, publish, react, comment, report, club isolation, my space, camp switch, account deletion), every test account deleted at the end.

## [0.1.0] — 2026-11-20

First public prototype.

### Added
- Home: live counters, Rich vs Poor match with tug-of-war bar, category tiles, wall of problems with filters and pagination.
- Join a camp in 10 seconds (anonymous session + non-blocking e-mail confirmation), magic-link reconnect, referral links.
- Publish a problem, with instant client-side filter feedback.
- Problem page: reactions, comments (delete / hide / report), reports with auto-masking at 3, share buttons, 1080×1920 story card, watermark.
- One club per camp (group chat refreshed every 4 s, camp isolation enforced in SQL).
- "Les chiffres" page, random problem, my space (camp switch, my problems, referral, rich wishes, newsletter, account deletion).
- Installable app (manifest, service worker caching static files only), help page, 404.
- Full demo mode (localStorage) with 60 fictional members; API contract test suite (Node + browser).
- Supabase: schema, RLS lockdown with zero direct grants, 20 SECURITY DEFINER RPC functions, moderation views and functions, seeds, SQL security test script.
- Tooling: static checks (syntax, unsafe DOM, absolute links, CSP, secrets in files and Git history, assets, JS budget), SVG brand generator, Open Graph URL sync, font wiring.
- Docs: README (EN/FR), architecture, configuration, moderation, roadmap.
