# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Who pays? / Qui paie ? to be a place where people laugh **at situations, never at people**. Contributors and members pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status (yes, rich or poor), nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and assuming good intent
- Joking about absurd situations, not about individuals
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Insults, harassment, trolling, or personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information (names, addresses, phone numbers, bank details)
- Asking for or offering money through the project
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and may ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for behaviors they deem inappropriate.

Report unacceptable behavior by opening a private security advisory on the repository (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) or by contacting the maintainer through GitHub.
